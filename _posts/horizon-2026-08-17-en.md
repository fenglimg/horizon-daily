rss · IT之家 · Aug 16, 11:37

In May 2025, Anthropic enabled AI Safety Level 3 protections with Claude Opus 4, which included deployment restrictions related to CBRN development or acquisition risks.

**「Impact」** The most concrete consequence is that Anthropic has strengthened screening and management requirements for external contractors after determining that their vetting processes were inadequate.
