### [Fudan team creates first single-layer CuO2 high-temperature superconductor](https://www.ithome.com/0/989/009.htm) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers at Fudan University, led by Professor Zhang Yuanbo, reported in Nature \(published online August 12 Beijing time\) the first successful fabrication of a copper oxide high-temperature superconductor thinned to a single CuO2 plane. The work provides direct experimental evidence that copper oxide high-temperature superconductivity is a strongly two-dimensional phenomenon. At the critical point of the superconductor-insulator transition, the team observed an exotic anomalous metal state and quantum critical phenomena. Using in-situ control of ozone concentration and temperature, they achieved a doping resolution of approximately δp≈0.0005, enabling observation of the complete phase diagram from the Mott insulating phase to the entire superconducting dome. The group plans to further explore higher-performance superconductors and advance research on two-dimensional quantum materials.

rss · IT之家 · Aug 12, 23:13

**「Background」** High-temperature superconductors, discovered in 1986, conduct electricity without resistance above the temperature of conventional superconductors, but their mechanism remains unresolved after nearly four decades. A long-standing hypothesis holds that superconductivity in copper oxides is essentially two-dimensional, confined to the copper-oxygen planes, yet testing this directly required isolating a single such plane without damaging its properties.

**「Impact」** By providing a clean, finely tunable single-CuO2-layer platform, the result gives researchers a controlled variable to systematically track how superconductivity emerges in copper oxides, potentially informing efforts toward higher-temperature superconductors and quantum computing hardware.
